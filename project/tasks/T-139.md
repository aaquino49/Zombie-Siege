# T-139: Night readability 2: zombie rim outline, glowing eyes, HP bars always shown at night (R-9)

Milestone: M6 | Type: code | Owner: gemini | Scenario: `qa_m3_breach`

## Goal

Owner request **R-9**, threat half. After T-138 lightens the night and adds warm light pools, make every zombie read
as a threat at a glance, anywhere on the night map, including outside the light pools: a 1 px light rim, glowing
eyes, and HP bars that stay visible at night.

**Depends on T-138** (it changes the tint). Read its captures and `work.json` first and judge against the new tint.

## Context

- `game/scripts/units/zombie_layer.gd` pools `AnimatedSprite2D`s (walker/runner/brute, 4× nearest, 16 px frames)
  and adds an `HpBar` child (`game/scripts/world/hp_bar.gd`) shown only while `0 < ratio < 1`.
- The sprites sit under `World/YSort/Zombies` and get the `DayNightTint` CanvasModulate like everything else.
- `HpBar._draw()` hides the bar at full HP. It is also used for survivors and buildings; keep their behaviour.

## Do

1. **Rim outline.** A small canvas_item shader, `game/assets/shaders/pixel_outline.gdshader`: draws a 1-source-pixel
   (4 screen px at 4×) outline around opaque pixels in a uniform colour, sampling neighbours with `TEXTURE_PIXEL_SIZE`
   so it stays pixel-exact. One shared `ShaderMaterial` per zombie type (or one for all), set once when the sprite is
   configured in `_configure_sprite`, never per frame. Outline colour: a pale sickly green (e.g. `#b8e07a`) at night,
   alpha 0 by day. Drive it with **one** global or shared uniform updated from the layer when the phase changes
   (not per sprite per frame).
   If the frames have no transparent margin for the outline, add `margin` handling in the shader (expand via
   `VERTEX` is not needed if frames are padded; T-102 padded frames, check `assets/pixel/frames/`).
2. **Glowing eyes.** Two 1-source-pixel red/orange eye dots that read at night. Simplest acceptable way: in the same
   shader, render pixels within the eye colour range at full brightness unaffected by the tint, OR a tiny unshaded
   overlay sprite per direction. Pick the cheapest one that works and say which in `work.json`. Optional if the rim
   alone clearly fixes readability; then log it in `project/assumptions.md`.
3. **HP bars at night.** Zombie HP bars show at night even at full HP (thin and unobtrusive, same 32 px width).
   Survivors and buildings keep "only when damaged". Add a `always_show: bool` to `HpBar` rather than a zombie check
   inside it.
4. The rim/eyes must survive the CanvasModulate: if the tint darkens the outline too much, make the outline colour
   bright enough or use a `CanvasItemMaterial`/shader `unshaded` path; verify by capture, not by theory.
5. **Captures** (1280×720, 1×) into `project/runs/T-139/a#/`: `qa_m3_breach`, `night`, `final_night`, `zombie_types`
   (day, to show the rim is off by day), `zombies_perf`. Contact sheet `night_threats_before_after.png` with
   T-106's `qa_qa_m3_breach.png` next to the new one.

## Acceptance criteria

- In the `qa_m3_breach` and `final_night` captures every zombie is clearly distinguishable from bushes and trees,
  inside and outside the light pools.
- By day zombies look exactly as before (no outline).
- Zombie HP bars visible at night at full HP; survivor/building bars unchanged.
- No per-frame material or shader-parameter writes per sprite (code review); `zombies_perf` shows no frame drop.
- New/updated tests: outline uniform is off by day and on at night; `HpBar.always_show` draws at full HP.
- All tests pass; import, smoke and screenshot clean.

## Pitfalls

- Don't edit `combat_fx.gd` (T-140 is changing it) or `day_night_tint.gd`/`night_lights.gd` (T-138).
- Pool reuse: the material must be set when a pooled sprite is configured, and death sprites need it too.
- A shader on a `TEXTURE_FILTER_NEAREST` sprite must sample with `texture(TEXTURE, UV + offset)`; don't add
  `filter_linear` hints.
- Keep the night "mood": a pale rim, not a neon glow.
