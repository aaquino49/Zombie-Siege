# T-022: M3 QA + headless night simulation test

Milestone: M3 | Type: qa | Owner: codex | Scenario: `default`
Depends on: T-019 (combat sim), T-020 (waves), T-021 (Game Over), T-041 (reinforced wall), T-068 (zombie view),
T-071 (roads), T-077 (combat view), T-078 (zombie priorities), and the M3 clean-ups T-072, T-073, T-074, T-075,
T-079, T-080, T-082 (resources kept off dirt), so QA checks the final M3 build.

## Goal

Prove M3 (Siege) is done: *nights happen, zombie waves attack, guards/turrets/towers defend with ammo, walls hold or
break, and you can lose* (GDD §§8–10, §14). Add a headless night-simulation test file, playtest the siege features,
and report bugs. **Don't fix game code**: report bugs instead.

## What to check

1. **Night flow:** nightfall tint and banner; the top bar shows "NIGHT – n zombies left" (counting pending spawns);
   the night ends only when every zombie is dead and the minimum night time has passed; then dawn.
2. **Waves (`WaveSpawner`, enabled by `Game.new_game()`):** night *n* spawns walkers 4+3n, runners max(0, 2(n−3)),
   brutes max(0, n−7), from 1/2/3/4 sides by night band, trickling in over the first 60% of the night, from map edges.
3. **Zombies:** they path to the CC along the shared flow field, detour short walls, break into long ones, attack
   exposed survivors within 1.5 tiles; runners chase exposed survivors; brutes smash structures first; zombies face
   what they attack (T-080).
4. **Defense:** guards man a free reachable watchtower (7.5-tile range) or a CC ring tile (5 tiles); turrets fire on
   their own; each shot costs 1 ammo; nothing fires at 0 ammo and the "No ammo!" warning shows; tracers and muzzle
   flashes appear and freeze when paused.
5. **Walls:** wooden walls take damage and are destroyed; Reinforced Wall upgrade (day 6) has 400 HP; builders repair
   damage by day.
6. **Lose:** the CC destroyed, or 0 survivors, shows Game Over with stats (nights survived, zombies killed, survivors
   lost, buildings built, time played) and Restart/Quit; the clock is locked.

## Deliverables

### `game/tests/test_night_sim_qa.gd` (new)

Use `GameState.new(GameData.load_default(), 1337)`, set `state.waves.enabled = true`, step with `state.tick(0.1)`.
Build the base with complete buildings placed directly (scan with `Placement.check(...) == OK` as other tests do, then
mark them complete) so builders aren't needed. Keep each test under ~15 s of wall time.

- **`test_defended_base_survives_night_1`:** a closed ring of wooden walls around the CC with a gate, 2 turrets and a
  watchtower inside, 2 Guards, start ammo. Run from nightfall of day 1 to the next dawn. Assert: dawn arrives within a
  reasonable cap (e.g. 300 s of sim time), the CC is not destroyed, `game_over` is false, every night-1 zombie was
  killed (kill counter = wave size), ammo went down by at least the number of kills × shots needed, and no survivor
  died.
- **`test_undefended_cc_takes_damage_night_5`:** jump the clock to night 5 (set `clock.day`/elapsed the way
  `dev/scenarios/night.gd` does), no walls, no guards, no turrets. Assert the CC loses HP during the night, and runners
  (night 5 has 4) appear.
- **`test_cc_destroyed_triggers_game_over`:** an undefended CC with low HP (or a late night). Assert `game_lost` fires
  exactly once with reason `"command_center"`, and `game_over` stays true on later ticks.
- **`test_night_sim_deterministic`:** run the night-1 defended setup twice with the same seed; kills, ammo and CC HP
  are identical at dawn.
- **`test_waves_disabled_by_default`:** a fresh `GameState` has `waves.enabled == false`, and `Game.new_game()` turns it
  on (if testable headlessly; otherwise note it).

If one of these exact flows already exists with a full `state.tick` loop (e.g. in `test_wave_spawner_qa.gd` or
`test_lose_condition.gd`), skip it and say which test covers it.

### Scenarios (`game/dev/scenarios/qa_m3_*.gd`, at least 4, with screenshots in `qa.json`)

Suggested: `qa_m3_night1_defense` (walled base mid-fight, tracers visible), `qa_m3_breach` (zombies breaking a wall
section, HP bars), `qa_m3_mixed_wave` (night 8: walkers, runners and a brute visible), `qa_m3_no_ammo` (0 ammo
warning, guards idle), `qa_m3_game_over` (the real loss path, not a forced screen).

### Reports

- `bugs.json` in the run folder (may be empty). Each bug has a title, the steps or test that reproduce it, and a
  severity.
- **Balance observations go in `qa.json`, not bugs**: e.g. how much ammo night 1 actually used, how long night 1 took,
  whether 2 turrets + 2 guards can hold night 5 and night 8, whether start ammo (40) is enough. These feed the M4
  balance bot (T-026) and tuning (T-027).
