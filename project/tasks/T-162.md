# T-162: Night forecast: a line under the top bar says what tonight brings

Milestone: M7 | Type: code | Owner: gemini (Codex stands in) | Scenario: `night_forecast` | QA: yes

## Goal

GDD §17.5. During the day and dusk, show "Tonight: 22 walkers · 4 runners · 2 sides" under the top bar so players
can decide in the morning whether to spend on walls, turrets or economy. It reflects the difficulty (T-147).

## Context

`game/scripts/sim/wave_spawner.gd` `plan_night(n)` computes per-type counts from `state.data.wave_for_night(n)` ×
`difficulty_multipliers.wave_size_mult` (rounded, with a minimum of 1 when the base count is ≥ 1) and the side count
from `wave["sides"]`. Which sides is decided by `_rng` at nightfall, so the forecast names how many sides, not which.

## Do

1. **Sim (pure, no RNG):** add `static func forecast(data: GameData, night: int, multipliers: Dictionary) -> Dictionary`
   to `WaveSpawner`, returning `{"walker": int, "runner": int, "brute": int, "sides": int, "final": bool}`. Make
   `plan_night` use it for its counts so the two can never disagree. **Keep the RNG call order in `plan_night`
   exactly as it is** (`_shuffle(sides)` first, then `_shuffle(types)`), so every seed plays out the same.
2. **View:** a `NightForecast` label in `game/scenes/ui/hud.tscn` (driven from `hud.gd`, or a tiny
   `game/scripts/ui/night_forecast.gd`), centred just under the top bar, on a slim themed backing (MenuStyle/theme
   panel), body size `UiFont` (22, never SMALL: T-159 found 11 px unreadable). Text from `HudText` if that's where HUD
   strings live: "Tonight: 22 walkers · 4 runners · 1 brute · 2 sides" (omit zero types, singular for 1). Night 20 (or
   `final == true`) reads "FINAL HORDE: …" in the red already used for the Final Horde banner. Visible during DAY and
   DUSK for `clock.day` (tonight = the coming night); hidden at NIGHT. Refresh on `phase_changed` and on bind
   (Continue starts mid-game).
3. **Tests:** `game/tests/test_night_forecast.gd`: forecast equals the counts `plan_night` schedules (count per type
   in `_schedule`) for nights 1, 4, 11, 20 and 23 on Easy, Normal and Hard; the label text for night 1 Normal and
   night 20; hidden at night.
4. **Scenario** `game/dev/scenarios/night_forecast.gd`: day 11 (walkers, runners and a brute), normal speed, 1280x720.

## Acceptance criteria

- Forecast counts match what spawns for every tested night and difficulty (unit test).
- Seed-1337 release-gate pin (`test_release_gate_qa`, 2682.00 s) and all balance-related tests unchanged.
- All tests, import, smoke and screenshot clean.

- Where `ShelterWarning` sits under the top bar: put the forecast where both can show at once, or stack them.
