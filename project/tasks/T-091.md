# T-091: Balance bot v2: sane job split, economy-first build order, loss row and night metrics

Milestone: M4 | Type: code | Owner: codex | Scenario: `default` (no visible change) | skip_qa: true
Depends on: T-026 (done).

## Why

The T-026 bot can't be used to tune yet. On all 5 seeds (1337, 1, 2, 3, 4) `sensible` loses on night 2 and `none`
on night 1, so every run shows the same thing. Two causes are in the bot itself, not the game:
- **Jobs:** with 4 survivors, `_jobs()` makes 2 guards + 1 builder + 1 farmer (as soon as the farm completes), which
  leaves **zero gatherers**. Dawn 1 shows wood 0, scrap 15 (untouched), ammo 12.
- **Build order:** `_decide()` places ring walls before anything else, and when a wall is unaffordable it `return`s.
  A radius-6 ring is 52 tiles (260 wood), so the watchtower and workshop are never placed and ammo is never made.

T-027 (my tuning pass) needs a bot that plays like a reasonable human and reports *why* a night went badly.
Change only the bot, its runner and its tests. **No changes to `game/data/*.json` or to any other sim/view file.**

## 1. Jobs v2 (`_jobs`)

Re-check every decide call. Sort people by `uid` and fill slots in this order. Only call `set_job` when a
survivor's job or gather kind actually changes (as today).
1. **Guards:** `G = 1` on days 1–3, `2` on days 4–7, `3` on days 8–11, `4` from day 12; capped at `survivors - 2`
   (minimum 0).
2. **Builder:** 1. Make it 2 while 3 or more blueprints are open and survivors ≥ 7.
3. **Crafter:** 1 if any workshop is complete and `ammo < 40 + 20 * day`.
4. **Farmers:** if any farm is complete and `food < 4 * survivors`: `ceil(survivors / 3.0)`, capped at
   `2 * complete farms`. Otherwise 0.
5. **Everyone else gathers.** The 1st gatherer gets wood, the 2nd scrap, then they alternate wood, scrap, wood, ...
   From day 8 (radio tower saving), alternate starting with scrap instead.

If the slots run out before a role is reached, that role gets nobody. Example with 4 survivors on day 1:
guard, builder, wood, scrap. Day 2 with a finished workshop and low ammo: guard, builder, crafter, wood.

## 2. Build order v2 (`_decide`)

Keep: at most 2 open blueprints, the ring planning (`_plan_ring`), the interior scan (`_place_inside`), never
building on ring tiles or `cc_ring_tiles()`, and the radio tower save rule. Replace the priority list with this,
evaluated top to bottom on every decide call. For each item that is **wanted and unlocked**, try to place it. If
placement fails because it's unaffordable, `return` (save up for it). If it succeeds, `continue` the loop.
1. Re-place destroyed ring pieces that were placed before (unchanged).
2. **Radio tower:** day ≥ 8 and none placed (unchanged: save for it, nothing else below runs until it's placed).
3. **Workshop:** none placed (unlocks day 2).
4. **Watchtower:** fewer than 1 placed (fewer than 2 from day 6).
5. **Farm:** none placed and (day ≥ 3 or `food < 5 * survivors`). After the tower is placed: a 2nd farm if
   `food < 3 * survivors` (unchanged).
6. **Shelter:** survivors ≥ beds and no shelter blueprint open.
7. **Lumber yard:** none placed (unlocks day 3).
8. **Turrets:** day ≥ 4, up to 4 before the tower is placed, up to 8 after.
9. **Ring walls + gate**, in ring order, one per loop iteration (skip BLOCKED tiles, as today).
10. **Reinforced walls** after the tower is placed and wood > 80 (unchanged).

Ring walls are now last, so they soak up spare wood: day 1 has nothing else unlocked and wanted, so the bot puts
its starting wood into the ring.

## 3. Metrics

Add these columns to `COLUMNS`, after `radio_tower`, in this order:
`end, dusk_ammo, dusk_wood, dusk_scrap, guards, turrets, cc_min_hp_pct`
- `end`: `"dawn"` for normal rows, `"loss"` for the loss row (below).
- `dusk_ammo`, `dusk_wood`, `dusk_scrap`: resource amounts at the DAY → NIGHT change of that night. Detect it in the
  run loop by comparing `state.clock.phase` before and after `state.tick(step)`. Store them in local variables that
  the dawn callback reads.
- `guards`: survivors with `Job.GUARD` at dusk. `turrets`: complete turrets at dusk.
- `cc_min_hp_pct`: the lowest CC HP % seen during that night. Sample it every tick while the phase is NIGHT, and
  reset it at dusk.

**Loss row:** when the loop ends with `state.game_over and not state.won`, append one more row with `end = "loss"`,
`night = state.clock.day`, current resources, `cc_hp_pct` = current value, and `kills`, `lost` and
`buildings_destroyed` from `state.night_log.summary(state)` (the same keys the dawn report uses). The loss happens
during a night, so dusk values exist. Normal dawn rows get `end = "dawn"`.

Return dictionary: keep the existing keys and add `"min_cc_hp_pct"` (lowest over the whole run).

## 5. Tests (`test_balance_bot.gd`)

Assertions must **not** depend on balance numbers, because T-027 will change `game/data/*.json` right after this.
Never assert an outcome, a night count, or exact resource amounts.
- Column order equals `COLUMNS`, and every row has every column.
- `run(1337, "sensible", 2)` with `on_dawn`: at the first dawn there is at least 1 survivor with `Job.GUARD` and at
  least 1 with `Job.GATHERER`, at least 1 ring piece is placed, and there are never more than 2 open blueprints.
- A unit test of the job split. Factor the slot maths into a pure static helper, for example
  `static func job_plan(day: int, survivors: int, ammo: int, food: int, complete_farms: int, workshop_complete: bool, open_blueprints: int) -> Dictionary`
  returning `{guards, builders, crafters, farmers, gatherers}`, and test the two examples from section 1 plus one
  day-12, 10-survivor case.
- Loss row: `run(1337, "none", 8)`. If the outcome starts with `"lost"`, the last row has `end == "loss"` and every
  other row has `end == "dawn"`. Otherwise every row is `"dawn"`.
- Determinism: two `run(1337, "sensible", 2)` give identical rows.
- The whole file stays under about 10 s. Never run 20 nights in the suite.
