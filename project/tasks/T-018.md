## Reference spec (unchanged from the original brief)

## Goal

GDD §8: zombies path to the Command Center, attack whatever structure blocks the way, and attack survivors within 1.5 tiles. Buildings and survivors take damage and can be destroyed or killed. This task is **pure sim** (`scripts/sim/`, headless-testable). It must hold **250 zombies** cheaply: one shared flow field and **no per-zombie A\***.

Out of scope: drawing zombies (T-068), guards and turrets shooting (T-019), wave spawning and runner/brute target priorities (T-020), Game Over (T-021). All zombie types use the walker behaviour in this task, with their own hp, speed, damage and attack rate from `zombies.json`.

## Data (add to `game/data/balance.json`, read with `data.balance(key, default)`)

- `"zombie_structure_cost_per_hp": 0.1`: flow-field cost of a building tile = `10 + round(type_hp × this × 10)`. Plain tiles cost 10 orthogonally and 14 diagonally. A wooden wall (150 hp) costs 160, about 16 tiles of detour, so zombies walk around short walls and break through long ones. They break the *cheapest* blocker (GDD §8).
- `"zombie_survivor_range": 1.5` (tiles): zombies attack a non-sheltered survivor within this distance (GDD §8).
- Don't change `zombies.json`'s existing keys.

## Rules

### Flow field (`game/scripts/sim/flow_field.gd`, new, `class_name FlowField extends RefCounted`)
- One Dijkstra over the 48×48 grid, **seeded from the Command Center's tiles** (distance 0). Each tile stores its integer distance and the index of its next tile (the neighbour with the lowest distance; ties broken by a fixed neighbour order, so it's deterministic).
- Tile costs: passable ground 10 (diagonal 14). Trees, rocks and scrap piles are **impassable** for zombies. Any building tile (complete or blueprint, walls, gates and every other type) costs `10 + round(type_hp × zombie_structure_cost_per_hp × 10)` using the **type's data hp**, not current hp, so damage never triggers a rebuild.
- 8-neighbour moves. A diagonal is allowed only if both orthogonal neighbours aren't impassable terrain (no corner cutting through trees or rocks).
- Use a small binary min-heap (write it in the file; Godot has no priority queue). A full rebuild must take **< 20 ms** headless.
- `_init(map: WorldMap, data: GameData, buildings: BuildingStore)`. It connects `map.changed` to a dirty flag (placing, removing and completing buildings, and trees running out, all emit `changed`). Rebuild lazily on the next query.
- API: `next_tile(t: Vector2i) -> Vector2i` (returns `t` itself on a CC tile and `Vector2i(-1, -1)` if unreachable), `distance(t: Vector2i) -> int` (-1 if unreachable), `rebuild() -> void`, `is_dirty() -> bool`.

### Zombie (`game/scripts/sim/zombie.gd`, new, `class_name Zombie extends RefCounted`)
- `uid`, `type_id`, `pos: Vector2` (world px), `facing: Vector2`, `hp`, `max_hp`, `speed`, `damage`, `attack_interval` (= 1 / attack_rate), `attack_timer`, `target_building_uid: int`, `target_survivor_uid: int`, `tile() -> Vector2i`, `is_attacking() -> bool`.

### ZombieStore (`game/scripts/sim/zombie_store.gd`, new)
- Signals `zombie_added(z)`, `zombie_removed(z)`. `add(type_id, pos) -> Zombie` (stats from `data.get_zombie`), `remove(uid)`, `get_by_uid`, `all()` (sorted by uid), `count()`.
- `damage(uid, amount) -> bool`: returns true when it kills the zombie, which is then removed. T-019 uses this.

### Behaviour (`game/scripts/sim/zombie_ai.gd`, new, `static func tick(state: GameState, dt: float)`)
For each zombie, in uid order:
1. **Survivor in range:** if any non-sheltered survivor is within `zombie_survivor_range` tiles (world distance), stop and attack the nearest one (ties by lowest uid). This beats structures.
2. Otherwise read `next = flow.next_tile(z.tile())`:
   - **Out of bounds** (spawned just outside the map, T-020): walk straight toward the centre of the nearest in-bounds edge tile.
   - **`next == Vector2i(-1, -1)`** (unreachable pocket): walk straight toward the CC centre, ignoring terrain, until the tile has a distance.
   - **`next` is a building tile** (including the CC, and `next == z.tile()` while on a CC tile): stop and attack that building. The zombie stays where it is (on its own tile, next to the building).
   - Otherwise move toward `next`'s centre at `speed × dt`, carrying leftover distance into the following tile like `SurvivorStore.tick` does. Update `facing`.
3. **Attacking:** `attack_timer += dt`. Every time it reaches `attack_interval`, subtract the interval and deal `damage`. If the target changes, reset `attack_timer = 0`. If the target is gone, resume moving on the same tick.
- Zombies don't collide with each other, survivors or buildings, and don't care whether their own tile is passable (a blueprint placed under one is fine).

### Damage (`game/scripts/sim/game_state.gd`)
- `var zombies: ZombieStore`, `var flow: FlowField`, and counters `var survivors_lost: int`, `var buildings_lost: int` (T-021's stats).
- Signals `survivor_killed(s: Survivor)`, `building_destroyed(b: Building)`, `command_center_destroyed`.
- `damage_building(b: Building, amount: int)`: `hp -= amount`. At 0 or below, a normal building is `buildings.remove(b.uid)` (this vacates the map, so the field rebuilds and T-056's code releases anyone sheltered inside), then `buildings_lost += 1` and `building_destroyed` is emitted. **The CC is never removed:** clamp its hp at 0, set `var cc_destroyed: bool = true` and emit `command_center_destroyed` once. T-021 shows Game Over.
- `damage_survivor(s: Survivor, amount: int)`: `hp -= amount`. At 0 or below, `survivors.remove(s.uid)`, `survivors_lost += 1`, emit `survivor_killed`. Make sure jobs and shelter code handle the removal (no dangling `work_uid` holds; other survivors keep working).
- `spawn_zombie(type_id: String, world_pos: Vector2) -> Zombie`: the entry point for T-020 and tests.
- `hostiles_remaining()` returns `zombies.count()` (update the comment: T-020 adds queued spawns).
- `tick` order per step: `evict`/Jobs (existing) → `survivors.tick` → `ZombieAI.tick(self, step)` → `clock.advance`.

## Tests (`game/tests/test_flow_field.gd`, `game/tests/test_zombies.gd`, new)

- **Flow field:** on seed 1337 every passable tile reachable on the map has a distance, the CC tiles have 0, and following `next_tile` from each map corner (if passable) reaches a CC tile in fewer than 200 steps. Tree and rock tiles are impassable, so `distance == -1`.
- **Detour vs. break:** a short wall segment (3 walls) across an open approach: the field routes *around* it (the path never enters a wall tile). A full ring of walls around the CC (place walls on every tile at Chebyshev distance 3 that is free): the path enters exactly one wall tile, and it's a wall, not a tree.
- **Rebuild:** placing a building marks the field dirty, and the next query reflects it. Damaging a building doesn't mark it dirty.
- **Rebuild time:** `rebuild()` on seed 1337 takes < 20 ms (use `Time.get_ticks_usec()`; print the number).
- **Movement:** a walker spawned 10 tiles east of the CC reaches the CC ring and starts attacking the CC within `10 × 64 / 40 + 2` seconds of ticks. Its hp is unchanged and `cc.hp` drops by 6 per second (walker attack rate 1.0).
- **Wall attack:** a walker whose only route is through one wooden wall attacks that wall; after 150/6 = 25 s the wall is removed, `building_destroyed` fired, `buildings_lost == 1`, and the walker continues to the CC.
- **Survivor attack:** a survivor 1 tile from a zombie loses `damage` hp per interval. At 0 hp they're removed, `survivor_killed` fires, `survivors_lost == 1`, and the zombie resumes walking. A sheltered survivor next to a zombie is never attacked.
- **Shelter destroyed:** zombies destroy a shelter with survivors inside, and those survivors reappear outside (T-056's removal logic).
- **CC:** with CC hp set to 10, a walker reduces it to 0. The CC is not removed, `cc_destroyed` is true and `command_center_destroyed` fires exactly once.
- **Out of bounds:** a zombie at `(-40, 600)` world px walks into the map and then follows the field.
- **Kills:** `zombies.damage(uid, 40)` on a walker returns true and removes it. `hostiles_remaining()` follows `zombies.count()`, and a night with one living zombie doesn't end until it's killed.
- **Performance:** 250 walkers spread over all four edges, 60 ticks of `state.tick(1.0 / 60.0)`: total wall time < 1.5 s (print the average ms per tick). Assert the flow field rebuilt at most once during those ticks.
- **Determinism:** two states with the same seed and the same spawns end with identical zombie positions after 300 ticks.

- The weak-reference callback pattern in `GameState._init` exists to avoid a reference cycle. Don't capture `self` in new lambdas stored on child objects.
- Use `PackedInt32Array` for distances and next-tile indices (`index = y * size + x`).
