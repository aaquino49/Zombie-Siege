# T-010: M1 QA playtest pass

Milestone: M1 | Type: qa | Owner: codex | Scenario: `default`
Depends on: T-005 (camera), T-009 (clock), T-044/T-045/T-046 (building rendering, BuildMode ghost/input, build bar: the split of T-040), T-037/T-038/T-042 (cleanups).

## Goal

Prove M1 is done: *"You can pan around a generated map, see resources, and place buildings that cost resources. Day timer runs."* (GDD §14). Exercise every M1 feature end to end, add regression tests for gaps, and report bugs. Don't fix game code. Report bugs instead.

## What to check

1. **Map:** seeded 48×48 map, the CC at the centre, trees denser at the edges, scrap and rocks present. The same seed gives the same map.
2. **Camera:** WASD/arrows/edge pan, wheel zoom 0.5–2× anchored on the cursor, never shows past the map edge at any zoom.
3. **HUD:** 4 resource icons and amounts match `GameState.resources`. "Day N" plus "Nightfall in m:ss" counts down, and the speed buttons reflect state.
4. **Clock:** Space pauses (the camera still moves), 1/2/3 change speed, the day→night→dawn transition increments the day, the build bar gains day-2 buildings at dawn.
5. **Build mode:** each day-1 building can be placed on free grass and costs exactly its buildings.json cost. Red ghost and no placement on trees, rocks, scrap, other buildings, off-map tiles, or when unaffordable. Shift-click repeats, right-click/Esc cancels, a wall drag makes a straight line and stops when wood runs out, clicks on the top bar or build bar never place.
6. **Readability (GDD pillar 1).** Look at every building at 1x zoom next to its footprint. In `project/runs/T-045/a1/qa_build_mode.png` the wooden wall draws as a thin plank strip that doesn't fill its tile, and the shelter reads as two narrow green panels. Report each building whose sprite doesn't clearly fill/represent its footprint as a `minor` art bug in `out_of_scope_bugs` (title starting "ART:"), with the sprite path and a screenshot. These feed the art task, they don't fail M1.
