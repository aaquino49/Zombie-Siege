# T-077: Combat view: tracers, muzzle flash, armed guards, turret aim, No-ammo warning

Milestone: M3 | Type: code | Owner: gemini | Scenario: `combat_fx`
Depends on: T-019 (combat sim: `GameState.shot_fired`, `Survivor.on_tower`, `Building.aim`).

## Goal

Make the T-019 fight readable: every shot draws a short tracer and a muzzle flash, guards hold guns, turrets point at
what they shoot, and the HUD shouts when ammo is out. GDD §11 "Feedback", ART_GUIDE.

## Work

1. **New `scripts/world/combat_fx.gd`** (Node2D `CombatFx` in `world.tscn`, above `Zombies`, below `FloatingText`),
   `bind(state)` from `main.gd`, connects `state.shot_fired` (method Callable).
   - Pool of 32 tracers (Line2D, width 2, colour `Color(1.0, 0.9, 0.5)`) + 32 muzzle flashes (Sprite2D with
     `res://assets/fx/muzzle_flash.png`, scaled to ~20 px, rotated along the shot). Reuse the oldest when the pool is
     full. No `new()` per shot.
   - Lifetime 0.08 s of **sim time** (`state.clock.scale(delta)`), so a paused game freezes the FX on screen (that's
     how the screenshot can show them). Fade alpha over the lifetime.
   - Start the tracer ~20 px out from `from` along the shot so it leaves the barrel, not the body.
2. **Guards armed:** `survivor_layer.gd` uses `survivor_<variant>_gun.png` when `job == GUARD` (stand sprite
   otherwise), rotated by `facing` as now. On-tower guards draw above the watchtower sprite (check z order).
3. **Turret aim:** `building_sprites.gd` / `world_view.gd`: for complete turrets, rotate the `turret_barrel` top
   sprite to `Building.aim.angle()` (+ the sprite's art offset; the barrel art points up) each frame.
4. **No-ammo warning:** HUD ammo label turns red and a small "No ammo!" label appears next to it while ammo is 0 and a
   guard or a complete turret exists. Static helper `HudText.ammo_warning(ammo: int, has_shooters: bool) -> bool`.
5. **Scenario `dev/scenarios/combat_fx.gd`:** reuse the `defense` setup, then tick in small steps until a
   `shot_fired` happened in the last tick, and pause, so tracers and flashes are frozen on screen.

## Tests

- `test_combat_fx.gd`: off-tree CombatFx bound to a GameState; emitting `shot_fired` 40 times keeps the child count at
  the pool size; a tracer becomes invisible after 0.08 s of sim time and stays visible while paused.
- `test_survivor_layer.gd` (extend): a guard gets the gun texture; switching back to builder restores the stand
  texture.
- Turret barrel rotation follows `aim`.
- `HudText.ammo_warning` truth table.

## Acceptance criteria

- Tests pass; all tests pass with no SCRIPT ERROR; import/smoke clean.
- `combat_fx` screenshot shows at least one tracer + muzzle flash, an armed guard on the watchtower and a turret
  barrel pointing at a zombie. Other screenshots unchanged except `defense` (armed guards, rotated barrels).
- No per-shot allocations in `combat_fx.gd`.
