# T-142: ART: ragged edges and texture for dirt paths and patches (no more flat dark rectangles)

Milestone: M6 | Type: asset | Owner: codex | Scenario: `map_overview`

## Goal

Since T-123 the paths and dirt patches are dark (#47352c) on light ground, which is right, but they now read as flat,
hard-edged black blocks. In `project/runs/T-107/a1/qa_default.png` the dirt patch near the CC is a solid brown L with
ruler-straight edges, and at night (`project/runs/T-138/a1/screenshot_qa.png`, `project/runs/T-139/a1/screenshot_qa.png`)
the same shapes look like holes in the map. Make them read as trodden earth: ragged pixel edges and a little texture,
same palette, same files.

## Context (measured)

- `game/assets/pixel/terrain/path_center.png` and `dirt.png`: 256 px of solid (71,53,44). `dirt_2.png`: 6 specks of
  (80,68,51).
- `path_edge_n.png`: rows 0–2 ground (148,153,116), rows 3–15 dirt, a perfectly straight boundary. The other edge,
  outer and inner corner cells are the same (straight lines, square corners).
- Code draws DIRT/ROAD via `PathTiles.path_tile_for()` and the 13-cell atlas `path_boundary.png` (208×16), built
  from the `path_<suffix>.png` cells. The recipe is `asset_library/ittaimanero/gapfill/terrain_light.py` (T-123),
  with a SHA-256 manifest.

## Do

1. Extend the T-123 recipe (keep it reproducible, deterministic, no randomness without a fixed seed):
   - **Edges:** replace each straight boundary with a ragged 1–3 px wobble (pixel steps, no diagonal AA). The wobble
     at each cell border must meet the neighbouring cell exactly, so pick the edge profile so it is identical where
     cells join (e.g. same profile at x=0 and x=15 for N/S edges, and corners derived from the two edges).
   - **Fill:** add sparse texture to `path_center`, `dirt`, `dirt_2` and the dirt part of edge/corner cells: a few
     #504433 specks and 1–3 small pebbles in a lighter palette colour (#77664e). Keep it quiet (≤ 10% of pixels); the
     ground must stay calm behind units.
   - Optional: a 1 px #504433 inner rim along the edge so the patch has a soft border at night.
2. Rebuild `path_boundary.png` (same 208×16 layout and cell order) and every cell PNG in place. Same names, sizes and
   binary alpha; palette-exact (only colours already in the T-123 terrain set). Update the manifest.
3. Captures (1280×720, 1×) into `project/runs/T-142/a#/`: `map_overview`, `default`, `night` and `final_night`, plus
   before/after sheets for `default` and `night`, and a zoomed contact sheet of the 13 cells tiled 3×3 to prove the
   seams join.

## Acceptance criteria

- `default` and `map_overview`: paths and dirt patches have ragged, natural edges and visible but quiet texture; no
  straight-ruler rectangles.
- `night` and `final_night`: patches read as darker ground, not as black holes; zombies and bushes on them still read.
- Seams: the 3×3 tiling sheet shows no gaps or mismatched steps where cells join; roads in `map_overview` are
  continuous.
- Palette-exact, same file names/sizes/atlas layout; recipe rerun is byte-identical. No code or data change.
- Import, all tests, smoke and screenshot clean.

## Pitfalls

- Don't touch grass, flower or tuft PNGs, props, or any `.gd` file (T-107 owns `world_view.gd`/decor, T-140 FX).
- Keep `.import` settings (nearest filter); don't re-save PNGs with a colour profile or palette mode that changes
  pixels.
