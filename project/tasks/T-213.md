## Framing already decided by the plan (keep unless you log a reason)

- **Morale:** each survivor has morale 0-100 with four named bands
  (e.g. Inspired / Steady / Low / Breaking), each with a pixel mood icon.
  Morale moves on events and at dawn. Effects: work speed for every job
  (stacks with Hungry's 50%) and guard/turret-crew accuracy (shots can now
  miss, so hits stop being certain at low morale). Breaking morale has a
  consequence: the survivor refuses work for the day, and two Breaking dawns
  in a row means they leave (mirrors the hunger rule). Clear player levers.
- **Tech tree:** a research building (do not call it "Workbench": it reads
  like the existing Workshop; "Field Lab" or "Radio Room" are fine) staffed
  by a new Researcher job, which produces research points by day. 8-12
  upgrades in 3 branches (Defense, Economy, Survivors), each with a cost,
  prerequisite(s) and one clear effect. It extends unlock-by-day: day
  unlocks stay for the base buildings and the **Radio Tower win condition is
  unchanged** (never behind research).
- Both features are data-driven (`game/data/morale.json`,
  `game/data/tech.json`), deterministic (no new draws from the game RNG
  stream unless the design says exactly where), and part of the dawn save
  as optional keys with defaults, so `SaveGame.VERSION` stays 1 and the two
  sim tasks can merge in either order.
