# T-054: ART: Footprint-readable sprites for wooden wall, gate, shelter, reinforced wall

Milestone: M2 | Type: asset | Owner: codex | Scenario: `qa_m1_readability`
Depends on: none. Absorbs QA bugs T-049, T-050, T-051, T-052 (dropped as duplicates).

## Goal

GDD pillar 1 is readability. In `project/runs/T-010/a1/qa_m1_readability.png`, four building sprites don't visibly fill the tiles they block:
- the wooden wall is an ~18 px plank strip,
- the gate is a thin pair of rails,
- the reinforced wall is another thin strip,
- the shelter reads as two detached green panels.

Players will think those tiles are walkable. That matters a lot in M3, when walls are the main defence. Redraw these four sprites in the existing Kenney top-down style (`project/ART_GUIDE.md`).

## Files (replace in place; same names, same pixel sizes, so no code or data changes)

| File | Size | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| `game/assets/buildings/wall_wood.png` | 64×64 | A solid wooden palisade or plank block that covers most of the tile (top-down: log tops or planks with a darker outline). It must read as "blocked" at 1x and 0.5x zoom. |
| `game/assets/buildings/gate.png` | 64×64 | Same wood palette and thickness as the wall so it sits in a wall line. It must be clearly different: e.g. a frame with a visible door or bars and a lighter centre. |
| `game/assets/buildings/wall_reinforced.png` | 64×64 | The new wooden wall's silhouette with grey metal plates and rivets, clearly sturdier. |
| `game/assets/buildings/shelter.png` | 128×128 | One unified roofed building (or a large tent) covering its 2×2 footprint, distinct from the Command Center (orange roof) and Storage (brown roof). |

- Walls, gates and reinforced walls are drawn the same way regardless of their neighbours. They must look continuous when placed side by side **horizontally and vertically**: fill edge to edge on all four sides, or use a symmetric design that tiles in both directions.
- Follow the ART_GUIDE pipeline: keep full-size originals in `asset_library/generated/originals/`, trim, then Lanczos-downscale, copy the finals into `game/assets/buildings/`, run `godot --headless --path game --import`, and update `game/assets/ASSET_INDEX.md` if it lists these files.

## Verification scenario

Add `game/dev/scenarios/qa_wall_tiling.gd` (typed vars):
- Place (with `try_place`, then `state.buildings.finish(uid)` if T-012 has landed; otherwise placements are already complete) a horizontal line of 4 wooden walls, a gate, then 2 more walls.
- Place a vertical line of the same, plus one 2×2 shelter, all near the CC and in view at 1x.
- Reinforced walls can't be placed from the bar (upgrade-only). Add one with `state.buildings.add("reinforced_wall", t, true)` for the picture.

## Acceptance criteria

- `wall_wood.png`, `gate.png` and `wall_reinforced.png` are 64×64. Their opaque silhouette covers at least ~75% of the tile height **and** width. The gate is distinguishable from the wall at 1x.
- `shelter.png` is 128×128 and reads as a single building covering its 2×2 footprint.
- `qa_m1_readability` screenshot: all 12 buildings visibly fill their cyan footprints, and wall, gate and reinforced wall are distinguishable.
- `qa_wall_tiling` screenshot: horizontal and vertical lines of walls with a gate show no gaps or seams between tiles.
- Import, all tests and smoke pass. No game code or `buildings.json` changes.

## Pitfalls

- Transparent PNG, no baked drop shadows that extend past the tile, and no text.
- Don't resize the other 8 building sprites.
