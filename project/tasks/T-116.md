# T-116: Balance bot v3.1: rebuild lost farms first, widen second-builder eligibility, tie guard cap to watchtower posts

Milestone: M6 | Type: code | Owner: gemini | Scenario: `default`

## Goal

Make the headless balance bot (`game/scripts/sim/balance_bot.gd`) play more like a sensible human, so balance numbers
reflect the game rather than bot blind spots. **Only touch `balance_bot.gd`, `test_balance_bot*.gd` and, if needed,
`game/dev/balance_run.gd` output.** No game data or sim changes.

## Context

- T-095 (bot v3) shipped with two documented narrowings:
  1. `job_plan` only adds a second builder from day ≥ 12, and `_jobs` counts only interior blueprints (workshop, farm,
     shelter, storage, watchtower, lumber_yard). It leaves out walls, turrets and radio towers to protect seed-1337
     pacing.
  2. The guard cap after day 12 is an unbounded `ceil(survivors / 4)` instead of the brief's `posts + 1`
     (watchtowers × guard slots + 1).
- The T-115 balance pass (decision entry 2026-10-11 in `project/decisions.md`, "For T-116") found three more bot
  blind spots:
  1. **The bot doesn't rebuild a destroyed farm** when its two blueprint slots hold the radio tower or wall repairs.
     The second-farm rule needs `complete_farms > 0`, so losing the only farm starts a starvation spiral.
  2. A second-farm blueprint can sit unbuilt for days behind the 150 s radio tower.
  3. The crafter stops at its ammo target with 300+ scrap banked (seed 2).
- Baseline (T-115, bot v3, seeds 1337 and 1–9, 20 nights): sensible wins 8/10 (seed 2 lost the CC on night 19, seed
  6 on night 20). `none` loses the CC on night 3 on every seed. Seed 1337 full run 2671.25 s.
- Command: `godot --headless --path game --script res://dev/balance_run.gd -- --seeds=1337,1,2,3,4 --strategy=both`
  (also `--nights=N` and `--out=path`).

## Do

1. **Farm first:** when food production is lower than it was (a farm destroyed) or `complete_farms == 0` after day 2,
   placing or re-placing a farm blueprint goes ahead of the radio tower and wall repairs. It may take a blueprint slot
   from a not-yet-started lower-priority blueprint. Relax the second-farm rule so it can count farm blueprints, not
   just completed farms.
2. **Second builder:** lower the day gate (try day 8) and count turret and radio-tower blueprints and damaged
   buildings (below 50% HP) as builder work.
3. **Guard cap:** `posts + 1`, where posts = completed watchtowers × `workers` from `buildings.json` (the
   watchtower has `"workers": 1`, one guard per tower; see `guard_job.gd` post logic). Keep a minimum of 1 guard from
   night 1.
4. **Crafter:** while scrap is above a reserve (e.g. 100 + the cost of the next planned scrap building), keep making
   ammo past the target, up to 2× the target.
5. Unit tests in `test_balance_bot*.gd` for each rule: farm re-placed first after a farm is destroyed, second builder
   on a turret or radio-tower blueprint, guard cap = posts + 1, crafter continues above the scrap reserve.
6. Run the 5-seed check above, then the full 10-seed check (1337,1..9). Paste the BALANCE summary lines into
   `work.json` with a before/after table against the T-115 baseline.

## Acceptance criteria

- Unit tests cover the four rules above and pass.
- 5 seeds (1337,1,2,3,4) `--strategy=both`: sensible wins ≥ 4 of 5, and `none` still loses on nights 3–6. The 10-seed
  table is in `work.json`.
- If the seed-1337 full-run time changes and `test_release_gate_qa.gd` pins it, update the pinned value only if it stays
  within the GDD's 40–45 min target. Justify it in `work.json` and append a one-line entry to `project/decisions.md`.
  If it leaves the target, revert that rule instead.
- Full test suite, import and 300-frame smoke clean.

## Pitfalls

- The bot must stay deterministic for a seed (no `randi()` without the seeded RNG).
- `test_release_gate_qa.gd` runs a whole game and is slow. The orchestrator watchdog killed it at 16 min once
  today. Run it once at the end, not in a loop, and use `--filter` for the bot tests while iterating.
- Don't tune game data here. If the bot now wins 10/10, report it; data re-tuning is a separate design task.
