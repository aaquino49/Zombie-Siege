# T-016: M2 QA playtest pass

Milestone: M2 | Type: qa | Owner: codex | Scenario: `default`
Depends on: T-012 (construction), T-013 (gathering), T-014 (farm + workshop), T-015 (selection/info/roster UI), T-054 (art), T-055 (unstick), T-056 (night shelter), T-061 (dawn rules), T-062 (cleanup).

## Goal

Prove M2 is done: *"Survivors walk, gather, farm, build, eat, sleep. Newcomers arrive."* (GDD §14). Exercise every colony feature end to end over several in-game days, add a headless multi-day colony test, and report bugs. Don't fix game code: report bugs instead.

## What to check

1. **Walk:** survivors path around trees, rocks, buildings and blueprints. Nobody ever stands on an impassable tile, including after a building is placed on them (T-055).
2. **Build:** placing a blueprint pays its cost. A Builder walks over and builds it (progress bar), and the building becomes complete. Cancel refunds 100% before work starts and 50% after. By day, builders repair damaged buildings and pay wood (`repair_cost_fraction`).
3. **Gather:** wood and scrap gatherers harvest the nearest node, carry 10 back to the nearest complete CC/Storage, "+10 wood" popups appear, depleted nodes disappear, and a Lumber Yard speeds chopping within 6 tiles.
4. **Farm and craft:** farmers make 3 food per farmer per full day; a farm holds 2 workers, the Workshop 1. A crafter turns 1 scrap into 5 ammo every `craft_time`, by day only, and pauses at 0 scrap. The info panel shows "Workers n/m".
5. **Eat:** at each dawn everyone eats 1 food, hungriest first. Unfed survivors are Hungry (half speed, labelled in the roster); after 2 hungry dawns in a row they leave.
6. **Sleep:** at nightfall non-guards walk into the nearest complete Shelter/CC with free beds (overflow to the CC), disappear and can't be selected, bank carried loads, and come back out at dawn on the same job. Guards stay outside.
7. **Newcomers:** at dawn, if beds > population and food ≥ 2 × population, 1–2 Idle newcomers appear on the CC ring with unused names.
8. **UI:** click a survivor/building for the info panel, change jobs there and in the roster (R), cancel a blueprint from the panel. Everything stays readable at 1280×720.

## Deliverables

### `game/tests/test_colony_sim_qa.gd` (new): the multi-day colony test

Use `GameState.new(GameData.load_default(), 1337)` and step `state.tick(0.1)` until `state.clock.day == 4` (3 full day/night cycles: about 5,850 ticks with the default 150 s day and 45 s night). Keep each test under ~10 s of wall time.

- **`test_default_colony_three_days`:** default start jobs (Builder, 2 wood Gatherers, 1 scrap Gatherer), no player input. Assert:
  - wood and scrap end higher than they started (60 / 15);
  - food never goes negative and ends at 30 − 4 × 3 = 18 (no farmer, no newcomers: CC beds 4 = population 4). If the rules legitimately give a different number, assert the computed value and explain it in a comment;
  - nobody is hungry and nobody left (`survivors.count() == 4`, every `hungry_days == 0`);
  - `dawn_processed` fired 3 times;
  - at the end of each night (the tick before dawn) every survivor is `sheltered`;
  - after every tick, every survivor stands on a passable tile (sample every 10 ticks if it's too slow).
- **`test_planned_colony_grows`:** before ticking, add a complete Farm and a complete Shelter near the CC (scan with `Placement.check(...) == OK`, as in `test_shelter_rules.gd`). Jobs: 2 Farmers, 1 wood Gatherer, 1 Builder. After 3 days assert: population > 4 (newcomers arrived: 8 beds, food ≥ 2 × pop on the first dawn), nobody left, food never negative, the farm produced food (track `resources` or the dawn reports: food eaten minus food change > 0), every newcomer is Idle with a unique name.
- **`test_starving_colony_loses_people`:** set food to 0, no farmers. After the 2nd dawn the hungry survivors have left (count drops), and the dawn reports list them in `left`. Skip it if `test_dawn_rules*.gd` already covers this exact flow with a full `state.tick` loop (not just `DawnRules.process` calls), and say so in `qa.json`.
- **`test_same_seed_same_result`:** two states with seed 1337 and the default jobs, ticked one full day each, end with identical resources and identical survivor tiles. (The M4 balance bot depends on determinism.)
