# T-188: Defender range preview when placing and selecting

Milestone: M9 | Type: code | Owner: gemini | Scenario: `defense` | skip QA (covered by T-190)

GDD §19.3. Turret range 5 and tower-guard range 7.5 are invisible: players
discover coverage by watching zombies chew walls. Show the range before
they commit and on the live defence.

## Scope fence

View-only. Touch the BuildMode ghost/placement view, the info panel
(range line for selected turret/watchtower/guard), `game/tests/test_range_preview.gd`
(new) and `project/assumptions.md`. No sim, data or balance changes; read
radii only from `GameData` (guard_range_tiles, tower ×1.5, turret
range_tiles).

## Do

1. While BuildMode holds a turret or watchtower ghost, draw a ground ring
   of radius `range_tiles * Grid.TILE` centred on the ghost tile (green
   when Placement.check passes there, red when not). Ring only, no fill
   that hides tiles; 2 px world-space line.
2. Selecting a complete turret, watchtower, or guard shows the same ring
   around the building (guards: around their post) plus an info-panel line
   "Range: 5 tiles" / "Range: 7.5 tiles". Hidden for blueprints and all
   other buildings.
3. Tests (off-tree, following `game/tests/README.md`): ring radius equals
   data × TILE for turret and tower-guard; hidden for farm/blueprint;
   colour follows a stubbed placement result.
4. `defense` capture shows a turret ghost or selected turret with its ring
   at 1280x720, readable by day.

## Acceptance criteria

- Ghost ring tracks the cursor, radius matches data, colour matches validity.
- Selection ring + info line for turret/watchtower/guard only.
- Rings never intercept clicks and never show at night over the tint in a
  way that hides zombies (thin outline only).
- All tests pass with no SCRIPT ERROR; import/smoke clean.

## Pitfalls

- World-space drawing must respect the ×4 pixel scale (draw in tile px,
  not texture px).
- Guard on tower uses the tower post position, not the survivor's offset.
- Keep LF line endings.
