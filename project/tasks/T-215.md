still returns its target from try_fire so cooldown/facing advance and
the ammo is spent once; no zombie damage/hit signal on a miss.

## Acceptance criteria

- Tests cover every mood trigger/value/lifetime (including sheltered,
  cancelled scavenging and newcomers not receiving last-night moods),
  same-tick stacks, caps, and 75/74, 40/39, 20/19 band boundaries.
- Farmer, Builder, Gatherer and Crafter work match band rates; Hungry
  Low yields ×0.425. Movement and guard cadence retain current rates.
- Same seed/day yields identical misses, ammo and damage; no target/no
  ammo and 100% shots leave combat_rng unchanged; state.rng untouched.
- One Breaking dawn refuses; two leaves (max one, morale then uid),
  departure moods do not cascade; last-survivor loss works. Moving,
  changing jobs, scavenging or feasting cannot bypass suspension.
- Campfire 6/7 slots, duplicate-fire cap, Feast atomic cost/cooldown/
  reason precedence, Day off 27.999/28 s and next-dawn mood covered.
- Old/partial/round-trip saves work exactly per §23.8; malformed
  optional values rejected. No expiry/reward replay on Continue.
- A scripted Steady fixture preserves old production/kills exactly;
  all invalid config fields have meaningful negative validation cases.
- Import, full suite and 300-frame smoke pass; default capture clean.

## Pitfalls

Use combat_rng only for sub-100% guard shots in uid order. Don't mistake
abstract bed entitlement for sheltered occupancy. Keep shared-file hooks
small and preserve T-216's independent load/save sections. No pin tuning
to mask feature behavior; T-220 balances. Test/architecture instructions
apply; keep LF and files near 400 lines (split rules if needed).
