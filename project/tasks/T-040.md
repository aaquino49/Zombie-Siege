## Goal

The player can pick a building from a bar at the bottom of the screen, see a green or red ghost snapped to the grid under the mouse, and click to place it. Cost comes off the HUD immediately. Placed buildings (and the Command Center) are drawn from `GameState.buildings`. This closes the M1 "place buildings that cost resources" goal.

## Acceptance criteria

- `build_mode` screenshot: the bottom build bar with icons, names and costs for the day-1 buildings. The placed shelter, farm and 3 walls are drawn with their sprites next to the CC, a green ghost wall sits on a free tile, and the HUD shows wood 95.

- The world draws buildings only from `GameState.buildings` (no hard-coded CC sprite left), and updates per building through the signals.
- New tests pass. All existing tests and smoke pass. The `default`, `hud`, `clock`, `map_overview` and `camera_zoomed` screenshots still look right.

## Pitfalls

- `get_global_mouse_position()` on a Node2D already accounts for the Camera2D. Don't redo the zoom maths.
- `_unhandled_input` only gets clicks that no Control stopped. Make sure the HUD root Controls that span the screen (if any) are `MOUSE_FILTER_IGNORE`, or every world click is swallowed.
- Shift state: `Input.is_key_pressed(KEY_SHIFT)` or `event.shift_pressed` on the mouse event.
- Building sprites from T-007 are 64×64 (1×1) or 128×128 (2×2). Scale by footprint anyway, in case one isn't.
- Keep each script under ~400 lines. Split the drag-line handling into a helper if `build_mode.gd` grows.
- Edge-pan still runs while building. That's fine.
