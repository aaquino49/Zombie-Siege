## Goal

On the title screen the guard's tracer should hit the zombie that is dying, not the one due to die 0.8 s later.

## Context

In `game/scripts/ui/title_scene.gd`, `_next_shot_target(shot_time)` computes
`fposmod(DEATH_TIMES[i] - shot_time, LOOP_SECONDS)`. `shot_time = _time - shot_age` often lands a floating-point hair
past the death tick (e.g. 6.0000000001), so the dying zombie's value wraps to ~14.4 and the tracer aims at the next
zombie. A replay of the capture clock shows 67 of 110 firing frames aim at the wrong zombie.

## Do

1. Snap `shot_time` to the shot grid (e.g. `0.4 + roundf((shot_time - 0.4) / SHOT_SECONDS) * SHOT_SECONDS`, matching the
   real constants) or add a small epsilon (`fposmod(DEATH_TIMES[i] - shot_time + 0.01, LOOP_SECONDS)`). Pick one.
2. Extend `test_live_loop_keeps_zombies_visible_and_shots_on_targets` in `game/tests/test_title_scene.gd`: on every
   firing sample, the tracer end equals the position of the zombie whose death age is in [0, 0.1).
3. Don't change any other constants or the composition.

## Acceptance criteria

- On every firing frame of the title loop, the tracer end point is the dying zombie's position.
- The new assertion fails on the old code and passes after the fix (say so in work.json).
- `main_menu` frame-120 captures still identical across runs and different from frame 240.
- All tests, import, smoke and screenshot clean.
