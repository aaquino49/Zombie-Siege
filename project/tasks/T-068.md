# T-068: Zombie view: zombie sprites, health bars and zombie scenarios

Milestone: M3 | Type: code | Owner: gemini | Scenario: `zombies`
Depends on: T-018 (zombie sim). Uses `GameState.zombies` (`ZombieStore` with `zombie_added`/`zombie_removed`), `Zombie.pos/facing/hp/max_hp/type_id`, and `zombies.json` `scale` and `tint`.

## Goal

Draw T-018's zombies, and show health bars on damaged zombies, survivors and buildings (GDD §11: "health bars on damaged entities"). After this task a screenshot of a night shows zombies walking in and chewing on a wall.

## Rules

- **Sprite:** `res://assets/characters/zombie_stand.png` for every type (already imported). Apply `scale` and `modulate = Color(tint hex)` from `zombies.json` (walker white, runner light grey 0.9×, brute dark red 1.5×). `rotation = facing.angle()`, the same as survivors.
- **Pooling (ARCHITECTURE rule 8):** a removed zombie's sprite is hidden and returned to a free list. A new zombie reuses a free sprite before creating one. The child count never exceeds the peak number of live zombies.
- **Health bar:** a small bar (about 32×4 px, green → red by ratio, dark background) drawn 24 px above the entity, visible only when `0 < hp < max_hp`. One `HpBar` (`game/scripts/world/hp_bar.gd`, `class_name HpBar extends Node2D`, `set_ratio(r: float)`, drawn in `_draw`, `queue_redraw` only when the ratio changes) used for:
  - zombies (child of each pooled zombie sprite; don't let it inherit the sprite's rotation, scale or tint: use `top_level` or reset its transform),
  - survivors (`SurvivorLayer`),
  - complete buildings (`WorldView`/`BuildingSprites`: width = building width − 8 px, above the building's top edge). Blueprints keep their existing progress bar instead.
- Draw order in `world.tscn`: Ground, Props, Buildings, Units, **Zombies (new)**, FloatingText, Selection. Zombies are tinted by the night `CanvasModulate` like everything else in the world.

## Files

1. `game/scripts/units/zombie_layer.gd` (new): `class_name ZombieLayer extends Node2D`, `bind(state)`, `sync()` (called from `_process`; positions, rotation, bars), pooling. Keep a texture/tint/scale cache per type (no `load()` per zombie).
2. `game/scripts/world/hp_bar.gd` (new).

4. `game/scripts/units/survivor_layer.gd` and `game/scripts/world/world_view.gd` (or `building_sprites.gd`): health bars.
5. `game/dev/scenarios/zombies.gd` (new, typed vars): default state at night (as in `night.gd`: set `clock.elapsed = day_length - 0.05`, tick into NIGHT). Place a line of 5 complete wooden walls 5 tiles east of the CC (`buildings.add(..., true)`, skip blocked tiles). Spawn 10 walkers, 1 runner and 1 brute just inside the east edge with `state.spawn_zombie`. Tick `state.tick(0.1)` until a wall is damaged and at least one zombie is attacking (cap 600 ticks), then pause. Centre the camera between the CC and the walls. Screenshot: zombies at the walls, a wall health bar, the night tint, "Night – 12 zombies left" in the top bar.
6. `game/dev/scenarios/zombies_perf.gd` (new): spawn 250 walkers spread over the four edges at night, tick 3 s of sim time, then zoom the camera out to 0.5 and pause. Print (don't push_error) the average sim ms per tick.

## Tests (`game/tests/test_zombie_layer.gd`, new, off-tree, per `game/tests/README.md`)

- Binding to a state with 3 zombies creates 3 visible sprites at the zombies' positions. `sync()` after moving a zombie updates its sprite.
- Removing 2 zombies hides 2 sprites. Adding 2 new ones reuses them (child count stays 3).
- A brute's sprite has scale 1.5 and the data tint. A runner's has 0.9.
- `HpBar` is hidden at full hp and at 0, visible at half, and its ratio follows the zombie's hp after `sync()`.
- A damaged complete building shows a bar. A full-hp building and a blueprint don't.
- A survivor's bar appears when `hp < max_hp`.

## Pitfalls

- Nodes in unit tests are never in the tree: no `_ready`, no viewport. Call `bind`/`sync` directly and free nodes with `free()`.
- Don't create Nodes or load textures per frame. `sync()` only updates existing nodes.
- The zombie art faces right at rotation 0, like the survivors. Check the screenshot and fix any rotation offset with a constant.
- Don't touch sim code. If you find a sim bug, report it in `notes_for_qa`.
- Static typing everywhere. Files under ~400 lines. Use only the Godot commands in `project/ARCHITECTURE.md`, always with `--path game`.
