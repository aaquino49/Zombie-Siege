# T-140: Pixel combat FX: muzzle flashes, blood hits, corpses and stains that clear by dawn

Milestone: M6 | Type: code | Owner: gemini | Scenario: `combat_fx`

## Goal

The FX half of the old T-107 (split for size and to keep lanes apart). Replace the old Kenney-era tracer and muzzle
flash with the pack's pixel FX, and give kills weight: a blood burst on hit, a corpse and a stain on death that
fade away by dawn.

## Context

- `game/scripts/world/combat_fx.gd` (`CombatFx`, node `World/CombatFx`, z_index 2) pools 32 `Line2D` tracers +
  `Sprite2D` flashes using `res://assets/fx/muzzle_flash.png` (old art). Driven by `GameState.shot_fired(from, to,
  is_turret)`; time is scaled by `_state.clock.scale(delta)` so pause and 2×/3× work. Tested by
  `game/tests/test_combat_fx.gd`.
- Pixel FX strips (16 px frames, horizontal) in `game/assets/pixel/fx/`: `pistol_muzzle_flash.png` (5 frames),
  `shotgun_muzzle_flash.png` (6), `blood.png` (5), `blood_stain_1..5.png` (single), `corpse_prone.png` and
  `corpse_seated.png` (3 frames each, flies loop), `bullet.png`. Mapping notes: `project/runs/T-101/a1/mapping_table.md`
  rows 76–84 (blood bottom-centre at the feet; never cycle the two corpse bodies into each other).
- `GameState.zombie_hit(z)` and `GameState.zombie_killed(z)` signals exist (`z.pos`, `z.type_id`).
  `zombie_layer.gd` already plays a death animation and fades the zombie sprite out (DEATH_SECONDS).

## Do

1. **Muzzle flash:** guards/watchtowers use the pistol strip, turrets the shotgun strip, at 4× nearest, rotated to the
   shot direction, played once over `LIFETIME`-ish (frame index from remaining time, not an AnimationPlayer). Keep a
   short tracer, but pixel-styled: 4 px wide (1 source px), pale yellow, no antialiasing; or the `bullet.png` streak.
2. **Blood hit:** on `zombie_hit`, play `blood.png` (5 frames) at the zombie's feet, pooled.
3. **Corpse + stain:** on `zombie_killed`, after the zombie's own death fade, leave one corpse (prone or seated,
   chosen deterministically from the zombie uid) with the flies loop, and a random-by-uid blood stain under it.
   Cap at e.g. 64 corpses and 96 stains (recycle oldest). All corpses and stains fade out during the dawn ramp and are
   gone by the end of dawn (listen to `clock.phase_changed`/`dawn`). They are decoration only, never in the sim.
4. Everything lives in `combat_fx.gd` or new helper scripts under `game/scripts/world/` (e.g. `fx_strip.gd` to cut a
   strip into `AtlasTexture` frames once and cache them). Pools are created once in `bind()`; `clear()` resets them on
   a new game. Corpses/stains sit below units (z_index 0 or a node under `Ground` level); flashes/blood above.
5. Delete the old `res://assets/fx/muzzle_flash.png` reference (keep the file unless nothing else uses it).
6. Update `game/dev/scenarios/combat_fx.gd` so the capture shows: a turret and a guard firing (both flash types), a
   blood burst, and at least 3 corpses with stains. Add `combat_fx_dawn` showing the same place after dawn (clean).
7. **Captures** (1280×720, 1×) into `project/runs/T-140/a#/`: `combat_fx`, `combat_fx_dawn`, `qa_m3_mixed_wave`,
   `zombies_perf`.

## Acceptance criteria

- `combat_fx` capture shows pixel pistol and shotgun flashes, a blood burst, and corpses with stains, all at 4×,
  crisp, no old Kenney flash.
- `combat_fx_dawn` capture: no corpses or stains left after dawn.
- Pools are fixed-size; no node is created per shot/hit/kill after `bind()` (test: child count stable after 200
  shots and 100 kills).
- FX respect pause and game speed (existing test style in `test_combat_fx.gd`).
- All tests pass; import, smoke and screenshot clean; `zombies_perf` shows no frame drop.

## Pitfalls

- **Do not edit `zombie_layer.gd` or `hp_bar.gd`** (T-139 is changing them). Everything hooks `GameState` signals.
- Rotation is allowed for FX (ART_GUIDE), never for characters.
- `AtlasTexture` frames must keep nearest filtering; build them once (static cache), not per event.
- The final horde kills 300+ zombies: the caps above matter.
