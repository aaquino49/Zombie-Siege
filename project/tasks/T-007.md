# T-007: Generate building sprites (Kenney style)

Milestone: M1 | Type: asset | Owner: codex | Scenario: `default`

## Goal

Top-down sprites for every building in GDD §7 that Kenney doesn't provide, matching the Kenney top-down look from T-001.

## What to deliver (in `game/assets/buildings/`)

- 2×2 → 128×128: `command_center.png`, `shelter.png`, `farm.png`, `storage.png`, `workshop.png`, `lumber_yard.png`, `radio_tower.png`.
- 1×1 → 64×64: `watchtower.png`, `gate.png`, `wall_wood.png`, `wall_reinforced.png`. Walls may reuse or tint T-001 wall art. If T-001 put a wall in `props/`, keep one canonical file in `buildings/` and update ASSET_INDEX.
- Construction overlays: `construction_1x1.png` (64) and `construction_2x2.png` (128), a scaffolding/planks look.
- `turret_base.png` and `turret_barrel.png` already come from T-001. Don't regenerate them.

## How

1. Try composing Kenney pieces first (roofs, crates and floors from the top-down-shooter tiles).
2. Otherwise generate with the ART_GUIDE prompt template, keep originals in `asset_library/generated/originals/`, trim, and Lanczos-downscale with PIL.
3. Each building must be recognizable at 1× zoom with a distinct silhouette or color: farm = crop rows, radio tower = mast/antenna, workshop = grey metal roof, shelter = tents or bunks, and so on.
4. Contact sheet at `project/runs/T-007/a<n>/contact_sheet.png`: every building on Kenney grass tiles at true size, with T-001 survivors next to them for scale. Regenerate anything that clashes (up to 3 tries each).

## Acceptance criteria

- All listed sprites exist at exact sizes, with transparent backgrounds and no text.
- Straight 90° top-down view; no isometric or perspective.
- The contact sheet shows a style consistent with the Kenney tiles.
- `ASSET_INDEX.md` and `CREDITS.md` updated (generated art credited as "AI-generated for this project").
- Import succeeds and tests pass.

## Pitfalls

- Generated images often come back with an off-white background instead of true transparency. Check the alpha channel and remove the background if needed.
- Watch for soft halos after downscaling. Check the edges against dark grass.
