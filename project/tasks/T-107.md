## Do

1. New `game/scripts/world/decor_layer.gd` (`class_name DecorLayer extends Node2D`), a child of `World/YSort`
   (y-sorted with units), bound from `world_view.gd` `bind()` after the map is drawn. Keep `world_view.gd` edits to
   that one call plus a hook in `_on_map_changed()` / building placement if needed.
2. **Placement rules** (pure static, testable: `DecorLayer.plan(map: WorldMap, seed: int) -> Array[Dictionary]` of
   `{tile, texture_path, size}`):
   - only on GRASS tiles with occupant NONE, never on DIRT/roads, never within 6 tiles of the CC, never adjacent to a
     resource node (trees/rocks/scrap stay readable);
   - wrecked cars: 4–8 per map, at least 8 tiles apart, preferably within 2 tiles of a road (they "broke down on the
     road"); all 3×3 footprint tiles must satisfy the rules;
   - small props (barrels, tires, signs, straw, bushes): about 3% of eligible tiles, in small clusters of 1–3;
   - deterministic from the map seed (use a `RandomNumberGenerator` seeded with `seed ^ 0x5eed`, not global `hash()`
     or `randi()`), identical across runs.
3. **Non-blocking:** decor is never in the sim (no occupant, no flow-field cost, no placement rule change). When a
   building or blueprint covers a decor tile (any of a car's 9 tiles), hide that decor sprite; show it again if the
   building is removed. Connect to `BuildingStore.building_added/removed`.
4. Same pixel rules: 4× scale, nearest, bottom-centre anchor so y-sort puts units in front/behind correctly.
5. **Captures** (1280×720, 1×) into `project/runs/T-107/a#/`: `map_overview`, `default`, `night` (decor must not
   make the night busier than the threats; dim is fine), `buildings_placed` (decor hidden under buildings), and a
   before/after sheet for `map_overview`.

## Acceptance criteria

- `map_overview` shows wrecked cars near roads and small prop clusters, adding variety; the CC clearing and roads
  stay clean.
- `DecorLayer.plan()` is deterministic (same seed → same list; different seed → different list) and never returns a
  tile on DIRT, on an occupant, near the CC or next to a resource node (unit test over 5 seeds).
- Placing a building over decor hides it; removing the building shows it again (test).
- No sim/data files change; balance bot results are unaffected (decor is view-only).
- All tests pass; import, smoke and screenshot clean.

## Pitfalls

- Don't edit `day_night_tint.gd`, `combat_fx.gd`, `zombie_layer.gd` or `hp_bar.gd` (T-138/T-139/T-140 are on them).
- Wrecked cars are 48×48: anchor them on their bottom-centre tile, and check the 3×3 footprint, not one tile.
- Keep decor sprites few (hundreds, not thousands): the map is large; cluster rather than carpet.
- Dark props on the dark night tint are fine as long as they aren't brighter than zombies.
