## Implementation

Set VERSION 4.0; same 5 s public decision cadence and fixed 0.25 s sim
tick; repeat harness jitters only decisions using its separate RNG.
Both played strategies keep the v3.2 farm recovery, forecast defense,
ammo and Radio Tower priorities. Exclude refusing/day-off/away/running
survivors from useful labor counts, prefer high-morale guards (tie uid),
protect last builder, food/crafting and a wood gatherer from reassignment.
Include Campfire/Lab blueprints in builder demand and preserve their
slots/progress on repeated same-job decisions.

Build one Campfire from day 2 if uncovered population exists and after
its 15 wood spend retain 30 wood; more fires only for uncovered slots.
Attempt a Lab from day 4 when completed Farm+Workshop+Campfire exist,
population >=6, after spending retain 60 wood/30 scrap, no urgent farm/
Radio Tower rebuild, and blueprint cap <2. Once day 8 unlocks Radio,
reserve its full missing cost before any optional Lab/fire spend.
Use existing interior placement scan, one extra blueprint per decision.
Staff one Researcher only from leftover gatherers with completed Lab,
at least 6 useful workers and food >=2×living population; keep one wood
gatherer. Staff a second only at >=10 useful workers, food >=3×pop and
ammo >= current bot target. Drop researchers before essential jobs
when short, restore them when conditions recover. These are bot policy
thresholds, not new game rules, and T-220 may not change them.

Fixed research order: camp_songs, spotters, better_tools, crop_rotation,
first_aid, oiled_turrets, ammo_press, reinforced_frames, bunk_beds.
At each decision with active empty choose the first unfinished available
id, preserving partial progress and bank; never switch an active id.
Feast only when mean living morale <40, command allowed, and food after
cost >=2×pop. Morning day off at most one per day: lowest-morale eligible
non-guard below20 (tie uid), only if >=7 useful workers, not the last
builder/crafter or food-critical farmer; re-read useful roster afterwards.
Scavenger send respects suspension and preserves researchers when no
other eligible spare exists; never mutate moods/RP/stocks directly.

Append CSV columns (retain old order): morale_mean (float, 2 decimals),
morale_min (int), morale_refusing (living refusal count),
morale_left (this dawn report.left_morale count, 0 on loss row),
research_done (cumulative count), research_active (id or empty),
research_progress (float, 2 decimals), research_banked (float, 2 decimals),
researchers (working unsuspended count), cc_damage (report amount for
that night). Populate them in regular and jittered/loss paths identically.
Summary may append final morale/tech values but keep old fields/names.
With no living survivors, morale_mean/morale_min/counts are all zero;
research columns still describe the retained tree. No division by zero.
Strategy none sends no command and gains no helper decision cadence.
