# T-212: Spitter CC-fallback standoff: fallback spitters must not siege the Command Center from outside all defence reach

Milestone: M12 | Type: code | Owner: gemini | Scenario: `default`

Gameplay bug fix for M12. Gated on the v1.6.0 export (T-210) so M11 ships
exactly what M11 QA certified; it then runs beside the M12 design task.

## Problem

T-207 traces (`project/runs/T-207/a1/README.md`, Diagnosis) showed: when no
turret or watchtower is within acid range, a spitter falls back to the
Command Center and parks at acid range from it. On many layouts that spot is
outside every turret's reach, so 2-5 spitters spit a full-HP CC to 0 with
ammo banked and every other zombie dead. T-207 papered over it with data
(turret range 6, acid range 2). A player can hit the same exploit.

## Scope fence

Touch: `game/scripts/sim/zombie_ai.gd` (`_tick_spitter` and helpers),
`game/tests/test_spitter.gd`, and `project/decisions.md` (matrix). No data,
bot, UI or view changes.

## Do

1. In the CC-fallback branch of `_tick_spitter`: if any completed turret or
   watchtower exists, the spitter does not stop at the first tile within acid
   range of the CC; it keeps walking the shared flow field until it is within
   acid range of the CC AND inside the reach (data range, fog-adjusted like
   Combat uses) of at least one completed defence, or until it reaches melee
   distance. With no completed defences, behaviour is unchanged.
2. Cache the "nearest defence reach" check per tick or per building change;
   no per-zombie scan of every building every frame (250-zombie target).
3. Unit tests in `test_spitter.gd`: (a) with a turret whose ring does not
   cover the CC-fallback stop tile, the spitter keeps walking and stops only
   at a tile inside the ring; (b) with no defences, the current fallback stop
   is unchanged; (c) existing priority, wall-bypass and fallback tests pass.
4. Run a 10-seed `--repeat=3` Normal + Hard sensible matrix (seeds 1337,
   1-9, 20 nights) with the T-211-fixed harness and record before/after
   majorities in `project/decisions.md`, moved pins old -> new. Do not retune
   data; the M12 balance pass owns that.

## Acceptance criteria

- A CC-fallback spitter never stops on a tile outside every completed
  turret/watchtower reach when such a defence exists; unit test proves it.
- No-defence fallback and all existing spitter tests unchanged and passing.
- 10-seed `--repeat=3` Normal + Hard matrices recorded in `decisions.md`.
- Full suite, import and 300-frame smoke clean.

## Pitfalls

- Deterministic: no new RNG draws in the sim path.
- The flow field is shared; do not give spitters their own pathfinding.
- Keep LF line endings.
