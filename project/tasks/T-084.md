# T-084: Rescue helicopter fly-in before the Victory screen

Milestone: M4 | Type: code | Owner: gemini | Scenario: `rescue`
Depends on: T-023 (win condition + VictoryScreen), T-083 (helicopter sprites).

## Goal

GDD §10: "A helicopter flies in, then the Victory screen shows stats." When the sim emits `game_won`, the camera
centres on the Command Center, a helicopter with a spinning rotor flies in from off-screen, hovers over the CC, and
only then does the Victory screen appear.

## Context

- T-023 added `GameState.game_won` / `won` and `VictoryScreen` (`scripts/ui/victory_screen.gd`), which currently
  shows itself as soon as `game_won` fires. It pauses and locks the GameClock.
- The game "pause" is the sim clock (`GameClock.paused`), not `get_tree().paused`, so Tweens and `_process` keep
  running while the clock is paused/locked. Good: the fly-in plays while the sim is frozen.
- Sprites from T-083: `assets/fx/helicopter_body.png` (nose +X, rotor hub at the canvas centre) and
  `assets/fx/helicopter_rotor.png` (hub centred). See `game/dev/scenarios/qa_helicopter_asset.gd` for the composite.
- `World` (`scenes/world/world.tscn`) has `Camera2D` (scenarios set `camera.position` directly) and a
  `DayNightTint` CanvasModulate.

## What to do

1. **`scripts/world/rescue_helicopter.gd`** (`class_name RescueHelicopter extends Node2D`), added as a child of `World`
   in `world.tscn`, after `CombatFx`, so it draws above buildings, units and zombies. Children: `Shadow` (Sprite2D of
   the body texture, `modulate = Color(0,0,0,0.35)`, offset about (+24, +32) px), `Body` (Sprite2D) and `Rotor`
   (Sprite2D, child of Body, at the hub). Hidden until the rescue starts.
   - View constants at the top: `FLY_SECONDS := 4.0`, `HOVER_SECONDS := 1.5`, `ROTOR_DEG_PER_SEC := 900.0`,
     `START_OFFSET := Vector2(900, -600)` (relative to the CC centre, off-screen top-right at zoom 1).
   - `signal rescue_finished`.
   - `bind(state: GameState, camera: Camera2D)`: connect `state.game_won` to `start()`.
   - `start()`: target = CC `center_world()`. Set `camera.position = target`. Show the node, put it at
     `target + START_OFFSET`, rotate the body to face the target, and Tween position to `target`
     (`TRANS_SINE`, `EASE_OUT`, `FLY_SECONDS`). The shadow follows. Then wait `HOVER_SECONDS` and emit `rescue_finished`.
   - `_process(delta)`: while visible, `Rotor.rotation_degrees += ROTOR_DEG_PER_SEC * delta`.
   - `skip()`: jumps to the end state (at target, visible) and emits `rescue_finished` immediately. It's for the
     `victory` scenario and tests.
   - Static pure helper for tests: `static func position_at(start: Vector2, target: Vector2, t: float) -> Vector2`
     (sine ease-out, `t` clamped 0..1). Use it from the tween via `tween_method`, so the tested math is the math
     that runs.
2. **`VictoryScreen`**: change `bind(state)` to `bind(state, helicopter: RescueHelicopter = null)`. With a helicopter,
   show on `helicopter.rescue_finished` instead of `game_won`. Without one (tests, old callers), keep the current
   behaviour. When the win happens, still pause and lock the clock **immediately** on `game_won` (so zombies freeze
   during the fly-in). Only the panel waits.
3. **`main.gd`**: bind the helicopter (`$World/RescueHelicopter.bind(Game.state, $World/Camera2D)`) and pass it to
   `$VictoryScreen.bind`.
4. **Scenarios**
   - New `rescue.gd`: the same win setup as `victory.gd` (factor a small shared static helper into one of the
     scenarios, or a `dev/scenarios/victory_setup.gd`, rather than copy-pasting). The screenshot is taken at frame 120
     (about 2 s), so the helicopter should be visibly mid-approach, rotor blurred, Victory panel not yet shown.
   - Update `victory.gd` to call `helicopter.skip()` after triggering the win, so its screenshot shows the panel
     straight away (the helicopter parked over the CC under the dim is fine).

## Tests: `game/tests/test_rescue_helicopter.gd`

- `position_at` returns start at t=0, target at t=1, a point strictly between at t=0.5 (closer to the target, since it
  eases out), and clamps t<0 / t>1.
- Off-tree: instantiate RescueHelicopter (or build it in code), call `skip()`, and assert `rescue_finished` was
  emitted once and the position equals the target. Read `game/tests/README.md` "Testing scenes" first: nodes aren't
  in the tree, so don't rely on `_ready` or Tweens in tests.
- VictoryScreen with a helicopter: the panel is not visible after `game_won` until `rescue_finished`, and the clock is
  paused + locked right after `game_won`.

## Acceptance criteria

- On a win the camera centres on the CC, the helicopter flies in over about 4 s with a spinning rotor and offset
  shadow, hovers, then the Victory panel appears. The sim clock is frozen from the moment of the win.
- `rescue` screenshot: helicopter mid-flight near the CC, no panel. `victory` screenshot: Victory panel visible.
- Tests above pass. All tests pass with no SCRIPT ERROR. Import, smoke and screenshots are clean.

## Pitfalls

- `DayNightTint` (CanvasModulate) tints the helicopter too. That's fine at dawn. Don't fight it.
- Don't use `get_tree().paused`. It would freeze the tween and DevTools.
- `game_won` fires inside a sim tick (during `GameState._on_dawn`). Starting a tween from it is fine, but don't
  free or reload anything synchronously there.
- Keep the camera's existing clamp logic intact. If setting `position` directly fights `camera_controller.gd`, use
  the same approach the existing scenarios use.
