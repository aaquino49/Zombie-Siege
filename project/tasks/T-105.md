# T-105: Pixel art swap 3: animated survivors (4-direction AnimatedSprite2D)

Milestone: M6 | Type: code | Owner: gemini | Scenario: `gathering`

## Goal

Owner request R-5 (graphics overhaul), third swap. Survivors are still the old Kenney top-down sprites, rotated to
face their heading. Replace them with the Ittai Manero pixel characters at 4×: animated, facing one of four
directions, never rotated, with distinct looks per variant, and y-sorted with buildings and props.
**View only. The sim doesn't change.**

## Context

- Read `project/ART_GUIDE.md` §2 (×4 at the node, Nearest, never rotate), §3 (bottom anchor, y-sort) and §4
  (animation names, FPS, fallback rules).
- View: `game/scripts/units/survivor_layer.gd` (`SurvivorLayer`). Today it makes one `Sprite2D` per survivor with
  `res://assets/characters/survivor_<variant>_<stand|gun>.png` and `rotation = s.facing.angle()`. HpBar is a
  `top_level` child. Scene: `game/scenes/world/world.tscn` (`Units` Node2D, separate from `YSort/Props` and
  `YSort/Buildings`).
- Art (T-102/T-118, already in the tree): `game/assets/pixel/characters/survivor_<variant>_<walk|hurt>_<down|up|left|right>.png`
  for the variants `blue, brown, green, old, survivor, hitman, soldier`. These already match
  `balance.json` `survivor_variants`, so **no data change is needed**. Also `player_*` (unused).
  Each strip is **48×16 = three 16×16 cells**, feet on row 15, horizontally centred. `right` is pre-mirrored, so
  never use `flip_h`. Per `game/assets/ASSET_INDEX.md`: walk plays cells 0-1-0-2 at 8 fps, idle = cell 0. Hurt cells
  are red tint / black silhouette / white flash, played 0-1-2 at 12 fps. Death = the down hurt cell 0, then a fade.
- Sim data you can read (don't change it): `s.pos`, `s.facing` (set by `survivor_store.gd` while walking and by
  `guard_job.gd` toward the target while shooting), `s.path`, `s.job`, `s.on_tower`, `s.sheltered`, `s.hp/max_hp`.
  Signals: `GameState.survivor_damaged(s, amount)`, `GameState.survivor_killed(s)`, `survivors.survivor_removed(s)`.

## Do

1. **SpriteFrames per variant, saved as `.tres`.** Write a small generator, `game/dev/tools/make_survivor_frames.gd`
   (`extends SceneTree`, run with `godot --headless --path game --script res://dev/tools/make_survivor_frames.gd`).
   It builds one `SpriteFrames` per variant from `AtlasTexture` regions of the strips and saves
   `game/assets/pixel/frames/survivor_<variant>.tres`. Animations, with `<dir>` in down/up/left/right:
   - `idle_<dir>`: cell 0, 4 fps, loop;
   - `walk_<dir>`: cells 0,1,0,2, 8 fps, loop;
   - `attack_<dir>`: cell 0, 10 fps, no loop (the muzzle flash comes from CombatFx; pixel FX are T-107);
   - `hurt_<dir>`: hurt cells 0,1,2, 12 fps, no loop;
   - `death`: down hurt cell 0, 1 frame, no loop (the fade is done in code).
   Commit the generator and the seven `.tres` files. Delete the default animation the generator gets from
   `SpriteFrames.new()`.
2. **SurvivorLayer uses one `AnimatedSprite2D` per survivor** (`scale = Vector2(4, 4)`, `centered = true`, no
   rotation ever). Place the node at `s.pos` and set `offset` so the feet (row 15) sit about 12 screen px below `s.pos`
   (e.g. `offset = Vector2(0, -5)` in native px). The node's origin is its y-sort key. Keep the HpBar child, the
   `z_index = 1` on towers and `visible = not s.sheltered`.
3. **Direction and state** in `sync()`, as small static helpers so they can be tested:
   - `static func dir_name(v: Vector2) -> String`: dominant axis, so `|x| >= |y|` gives right/left, else down/up.
     A zero vector keeps the previous direction.
   - Moving = `s.pos` changed since the last sync (store the last pos per uid), or `s.path` isn't empty. Moving plays
     `walk_<dir>` from the movement delta. Standing plays `idle_<dir>` from `s.facing`. A guard whose `fire_timer` was
     just reset (or use `GameState.shot_fired` with `from` ≈ `s.pos`) plays `attack_<dir>` toward `s.facing`, once.
   - Only call `play()` when the animation name changes, so a looping walk doesn't restart every frame.
4. **Hurt:** connect `survivor_damaged`. Play `hurt_<dir>` once, then return to idle or walk (`animation_finished`).
   Movement must not interrupt the hurt animation while it plays.
5. **Death:** on `survivor_killed`, before the sim removes the survivor, leave a short-lived `AnimatedSprite2D` at the
   same place playing `death`, tween `modulate.a` to 0 over 0.5 s, then `queue_free`. It must not block
   `survivor_removed` cleanup. It must be freed by `clear()`/unbind, so no leaked tweens or nodes at exit.
6. **Y-sort with buildings:** move `Units` under `YSort` in `world.tscn` and set `y_sort_enabled = true` on it. A
   survivor walking north of a building is then drawn behind it, and south of it in front. Leave `Zombies` where it
   is; T-106 moves it.
7. **Guards:** the pack has no gun pose. A guard looks like its variant, faces its target and shows the existing
   muzzle flash. Log this in `project/assumptions.md`. Remove the `_stand`/`_gun` texture switching. **Don't delete**
   the old `game/assets/characters/` files (old QA scenarios still load them; they are removed at the end of M6).
8. **Selection ring and job/carry indicators** (check `selection_controller.gd` and anything that reads SurvivorLayer
   sprites) stay centred on the survivor. If something looks up the survivor's `Sprite2D`, update it to
   `AnimatedSprite2D` or to a plain `Node2D` lookup.
9. **Tests:** rewrite `game/tests/test_survivor_layer.gd` for the new contract:
   - bind creates one `AnimatedSprite2D` per survivor with scale 4 and `rotation == 0` after sync, for any facing;
   - `dir_name` covers all four directions and the diagonal tie;
   - moving gives `walk_<dir>`, standing gives `idle_<dir>` from facing, and a damaged signal gives `hurt_<dir>`;
   - each variant in `survivor_variants` gets the frames for its variant (the `sprite_frames.resource_path` contains
     the variant), and every one of the 7 `.tres` files has all 21 animations with the right frame counts;
   - the death sprite is freed after `clear()`; removing a survivor removes its node.
   Update `test_shelter_rules.gd`, `test_zombie_layer.gd` and `test_zombie_view_qa.gd` only where they use the old
   `Sprite2D`/texture API. Keep their intent.
10. **Captures** (before = first, after = when done) into `project/runs/T-105/a#/`: `gathering`, `defense`,
    `night_shelter`, `construction`. Mention them in `notes_for_qa`.

## Acceptance criteria

- `gathering` and `defense` captures show pixel survivors at 4× with distinct looks per variant. They face their
  movement or their target, never rotated, and the pixels are crisp.
- Walking plays the walk cycle, standing is idle, damage plays the hurt flash, and death fades out in about 0.5 s
  with no leaked nodes.
- Survivors y-sort with buildings and props (behind a building to the north, in front to the south).
- HP bars, the selection ring and tower z-order still line up. Sheltered survivors stay hidden.
- Sim unchanged: all sim tests pass untouched. The rewritten view tests above pass. All tests pass; import, smoke and
  screenshot clean (no `ERROR:` lines, no leaked instances at exit).

## Pitfalls

- `AnimatedSprite2D.offset` is in unscaled texture pixels; negative y moves the sprite up.
- `play("walk_down")` every frame restarts the animation. Compare with `animation` first.
- `SpriteFrames.new()` comes with a `default` animation. Remove it or name-clash errors follow.
- Don't keep SpriteFrames in a `static var` cache. Load the `.tres` with `load()` (Godot caches resources), so
  nothing outlives the tree at exit (see T-119).
- Tests run outside the tree: `_process` doesn't run and tweens don't advance. Call `sync()` directly, and make
  `clear()` free death sprites synchronously.
- The hurt strips' cell 1 is a black silhouette. That's intended: it's the flash.
