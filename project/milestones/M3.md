## What works
- **Day/night** (T-017): a night tint ramp and a NIGHT n / FINAL HORDE banner. A night lasts at least 45 s and ends when the wave is cleared.
- **Zombies** (T-018/T-068/T-073/T-074/T-078/T-080): one shared flow field from the CC. Structures are passable at a high cost, so zombies breach the cheapest wall. Walkers, runners (chase survivors) and brutes (smash structures). Pooled sprites with HP bars, and zombies face what they attack.
- **Combat** (T-019/T-077/T-079): guards post on watchtowers (only reachable ones) or by the CC. Turrets auto-fire, and each shot costs 1 ammo. Tracers, muzzle flash, turret aim, and a "No ammo!" warning.
- **Waves** (T-020): GDD §9 formula from `waves.json`, spawned over 60% of the night from 1–4 sides, with the final horde on night 20. A seeded RNG stream keeps it deterministic.
- **Walls** (T-041): Wooden Wall → Reinforced Wall upgrade through the info panel. Builders repair by day.
- **Losing** (T-021): CC destroyed, or no survivors left → Game Over panel with five stats, and a locked clock.
- **Map polish** (T-071/T-075/T-082): winding dirt roads and soft dirt patches with grass edges. No resources on dirt.
- **Health** (T-072): GameState and all its stores free cleanly (a reference-cycle regression test), a prerequisite for Restart in M4.
- **M3 QA** (T-022): five full-tick night simulations (a defended night 1 survives, an undefended night-5 CC takes damage, a real CC loss fires game_lost once, same seed = same night, waves are on in a real game). Six siege scenarios. 387 tests passed at close.
