## Done when (GDD §14)
"Survivors walk, gather, farm, build, eat, sleep. Newcomers arrive." **Met.**

## What works
- **Survivors** (T-011/T-055/T-066): 4 named survivors with sprites walk the grid with A* (`Pathfinder`). Anyone covered by a new building gets nudged to the nearest free tile and goes back to work.
- **Construction** (T-012/T-058): placing a building creates a blueprint. Builders work from the ring around it, cancel refunds 100% or 50%, and damaged buildings get repaired.
- **Gathering** (T-013/T-059): wood and scrap gatherers harvest, carry 10, and deliver to the nearest CC or Storage. Depleted trees disappear.
- **Farmers and crafters** (T-014): farms make food by day, and the Workshop turns 1 scrap into 5 ammo.
- **Dawn rules** (T-061): the hungriest eat first, hungry survivors slow down, starving ones leave, and newcomers arrive if there are free beds.
- **Night shelter** (T-056): non-guards go to bed at nightfall, are hidden while inside, and come out at dawn.
- **UI** (T-015/T-047/T-060/T-067): click to select, info panel (HP, state, workers), survivor roster with job dropdowns (R), and a build bar that grows to 10 buttons by day 8 without being covered.
- **M2 QA** (T-016): a multi-day colony test. The default colony survives 3 days, a planned colony grows to 8, a starving colony dies out after two hungry dawns, and the same seed gives the same result. 285 tests passed at close.
