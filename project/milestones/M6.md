## What works
- **Pixel art everywhere** (owner request R-5; T-100 to T-107, T-118, T-120, T-123, T-142): terrain with light
  ground and ragged dark dirt paths, pack buildings, walls, fences and gates (both orientations), 4-direction animated
  survivors (distinct looks) and walker/runner/brute zombies, decor props.
- **Themed pixel UI** (T-108, T-109, T-122, T-130 to T-132, T-141, T-143): one Godot Theme, crisp text at multiples
  of 11 px (owner R-7), the numeral-distinct Siege Pixel font (owner R-8), icon + 22 px build-bar costs.
- **Menus** (T-110 to T-112, T-135, T-154): restyled main, pause, Dawn Report, Game Over and Victory with fades; new
  Settings, How to Play and Credits screens; a live scripted siege scene behind the title.
- **Night readability** (owner R-9; T-138, T-139): lighter night tint, warm light pools, a red Final Horde, a pale rim
  and glowing eyes on zombies, HP bars all night.
- **Combat FX** (T-140): pistol and shotgun flashes, blood, corpses and stains that clear by dawn.
- **Balance** (T-115, T-116, T-128, T-136): bot v3.1; sensible 9 won, 1 survived, 0 lost on 10 seeds; seed-1337 run
  44:42 at 1x.
- **Stability chores:** shutdown leak fixed (T-119, T-134), flaky timing test fixed (T-133), the menu-flow QA scripts
  reliable headless and with fades (T-153, T-156, T-160).
- **Shipped early from M7** (each passed its own QA): dawn autosave + Continue (T-144, T-146), CC alarm + screen shake
  (T-145), build info card (T-152).
- **Release:** `builds/ZombieSiege.exe` at 1.1.0 (file/product 1.1.0.0), 680 tests passing, clean import, smoke, exe
  smoke and a working autosave from the exe (T-114).
