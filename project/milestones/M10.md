## What works

- **Weather sim** (T-193, T-200): day Clear/Rain, night Clear/Fog from
  their own seed+day RNG (never touches the game stream, same on
  Restart/Continue, nothing new in the save). Rain: farms +50% from
  day 2 at 15%. Fog: defence range -1 tile from night 5 at 10%,
  never night 20, never twice in a row.
- **Weather view** (T-194, T-202): rain streaks, six broad fog
  patches capped at 0.23 alpha, top-bar rain tag, fogged range rings
  and info-panel range lines.
- **Idle survivors button + I hotkey** (T-195): top-bar Idle:N by
  day, cycles idle survivors in roster order.
- **Personal records** (T-196, T-201): per-difficulty best nights,
  wins, fastest win in `user://records.cfg`; New-record lines and
  a menu Best line; Victory panel fits 1280x720 with all three
  record lines.
- **Balance** (T-197): fog 15%→10%, rain 20%→15%. Normal sensible
  6/3/1, Hard 3/0/7, `none` loses night 4 everywhere. Seed-1337
  wins again at 2709.50 s (45:09.5 at 1x, 9.5 s over the §1 band,
  accepted as "about"). Missed targets by one win each side: rain
  moves the bot through noise, not weakness (decisions.md).
- **Release:** `builds/ZombieSiege.exe` 1.5.0, 839 tests passing,
  clean import/smoke, exe smoke + dawn autosave check (T-199).
