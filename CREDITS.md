## T-007 building sprites

All 13 building and construction sprites are composed from **Kenney Top-Down Shooter (CC0 1.0)** pieces: floors, wood rails, crates, leafy vegetables, metal panels, round fixtures and equipment. Project adaptations use cropping, flat recoloring, rotation and Lanczos resizing; exact source filenames are listed in `game/assets/ASSET_INDEX.md` and the reproducible pipeline is `project/tools/compose_buildings.py`. `wall_wood.png` is relocated unchanged to `game/assets/buildings/`; the T-001 turret base and barrel are unchanged. ## T-083 rescue helicopter sprites

The helicopter fuselage and separate main rotor were AI-generated by **Codex using OpenAI's built-in image_gen** for Zombie Siege, following the project's Kenney-style art direction. These are original generated images rather than Kenney pack assets. Transparent full-resolution sources and prompt/processing notes are retained in `asset_library/generated/originals/t083_helicopter_*`; `project/tools/prepare_helicopter.py` performs pivot-centred trimming and Lanczos downscaling, retaining the source alpha and multiplying rotor alpha by 0.65 for a spinning appearance. The contact sheet's grass, Command Center, radio tower, tree, turret and survivor comparisons use the credited Kenney CC0 assets above. No additional downloaded artwork is used.
